01 / ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY

A simple way to get involved in real estate marketing.

Property Scout Network connects motivated individuals with opportunities to assist in promoting available rental properties.

Scouts help share approved property listings across social media platforms and direct interested renters to our real estate team.

02 / THE PROCESS

Getting started is straightforward.

Understand the process before submitting your application.

Submit an application

02

03

Approved scouts receive instructions for sharing authorized rental advertisements.

04

Connect renters

Direct interested renters to our team for all property inquiries and leasing matters.

03 / WHO CAN APPLY

We're looking for people who are ready to get involved.

You don't need to be a real estate professional to express interest. We welcome applicants with good communication skills and an understanding of social media.

  • Comfortable using social media platforms
  • Familiarity with online property advertisements
  • Good communication and organizational skills
  • Reliable and able to follow instructions
  • Willingness to follow advertising and platform policies
